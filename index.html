Problem Identified

Fashion Decision Fatigue

Users spend 20+ min daily deciding what to wear. Existing apps add inspiration — which adds options, not reduces friction. Real JTBD: "Tell me if this outfit is good right now."

What Users Love

Brutal Honesty

Users consistently say two things: no fake compliments and zero manual effort. Early users requesting premium features — signals willingness to pay. V2 in progress.

Human-in-Loop

AI Suggests, Human Decides

Ratings are AI-generated but users always override the final call. We keep judgment with the human — AI makes the first suggestion faster. Core product design principle.

What's Next

V2 In Progress

Personalised recommendations, premium monetisation, deeper wardrobe insights. Validating demand from early users before building — no features shipped without signal.
